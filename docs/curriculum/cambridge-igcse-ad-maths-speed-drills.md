# Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606) Speed & Mental Maths Roadmap

This guide outlines higher-difficulty mathematical topics aligned with the **Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606)** syllabus, designed to build rapid mental calculation, algebraic fluency, and mathematical intuition under timed exam conditions.

---

## 1. Syllabus Progression & Speed Drill Candidates

Below is the structured topic progression from foundational IGCSE (0580) bridging into Additional Mathematics (0606), organized in increasing order of difficulty.

```
Foundations (IGCSE 0580)
  │
  ├── 1. Indices & Surds (Mental Powers & Conjugate Multiplication)
  ├── 2. Quadratic Functions & Inequalities (Roots, Vertex, Discriminants)
  ├── 3. Simultaneous Equations (Linear + Quadratic Elimination)
  │
Intermediate Ad Maths (0606 Core)
  │
  ├── 4. Logarithmic & Exponential Equations (Log laws & Base evaluation)
  ├── 5. Remainder & Factor Theorem (Synthetic substitution & Polynomial roots)
  ├── 6. Circular Measure (Radians, Sector areas, Arc lengths)
  ├── 7. Coordinate Geometry & Linear Law (Perpendicular gradients, Y = mX + c reduction)
  ├── 8. Trigonometric Exact Values & Identities (Special angles in radians)
  │
Advanced Ad Maths (0606 Distinction Level)
  │
  ├── 9. Binomial Expansions & Series (AP & GP Sum to Infinity)
  ├── 10. Permutations & Combinations (Fast factorial arithmetic)
  ├── 11. Vectors in 2D (Magnitudes, dot products, colinearity)
  ├── 12. Differentiation (Mental power/chain rules, gradients of tangents)
  └── 13. Integration (Definite integrals, area under polynomial curves)
```

## 2. Topic Breakdown & Mental Speed Shortcuts

### Topic 1: Indices and Surds (0606 Topic 4)
* **What trips students up:** Fractional exponents and rationalising surd denominators.
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$, $a^{m/n} = (\sqrt[n]{a})^m$. E.g., $27^{2/3} = (\sqrt[3]{27})^2 = 3^2 = 9$.
  - Conjugate multiplication for $\frac{k}{\sqrt{a} + b}$: mental difference of squares $(a - b^2)$ in the denominator.
  - Converting to prime bases: $8^x \cdot 4^2 = 2^{3x+4}$.
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `Evaluate 16^(3/4)` $\to$ `8`
  - `Evaluate 125^(-2/3)` $\to$ `1/25 = 0.04`
  - `Simplify √75 − √12 as k√3. Find k` $\to$ `5 - 2 = 3`
  - `Rationalise 6 / (√5 + 1) as a√5 - b. Find a` $\to$ `6/(5-1) = 1.5`

---

### Topic 2: Logarithmic and Exponential Functions (0606 Topic 7)
* **What trips students up:** Switching between $\log_a b = c \iff a^c = b$, change of base formula, and solving quadratic equations in disguise (e.g. $2^{2x} - 5(2^x) + 4 = 0$).
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - $\log_a(x) + \log_a(y) = \log_a(xy)$, $\log_a(x) - \log_a(y) = \log_a(x/y)$.
  - Power rule: $\log_a(x^k) = k \log_a(x)$.
  - Base inversion: $\log_a(b) = \frac{1}{\log_b(a)}$.
  - $\ln(e^k) = k$, $e^{\ln k} = k$.
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `Evaluate log₂(64)` $\to$ `6`
  - `Evaluate log₃(81√3)` $\to$ `4.5`
  - `Evaluate log₁₀(25) + log₁₀(4)` $\to$ `log₁₀(100) = 2`
  - `Solve for x: 3^(2x - 1) = 27` $\to$ `2x - 1 = 3 ⟹ x = 2`
  - `Solve for x: log₂(x) + log₂(x - 6) = 4` $\to$ `x(x-6)=16 ⟹ x = 8`

---

### Topic 3: Polynomial Remainder and Factor Theorem (0606 Topic 5)
* **What trips students up:** Long polynomial division when only the remainder is needed.
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - Remainder theorem: Remainder of $P(x) \div (x - c)$ is simply $P(c)$.
  - For $(ax - b)$, evaluate $P(b/a)$.
  - If $(x - c)$ is a factor, $P(c) = 0$.
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `Remainder when x³ - 4x² + 5x - 2 is divided by x - 3?` $\to$ `27 - 36 + 15 - 2 = 4`
  - `If x - 2 is a factor of x³ + kx - 14, find k` $\to$ `8 + 2k - 14 = 0 ⟹ 2k = 6 ⟹ k = 3`
  - `Remainder when 2x³ + x² - 8 is divided by x + 2?` $\to$ `2(-8) + 4 - 8 = -20`

---

### Topic 4: Circular Measure (Radians) (0606 Topic 9)
* **What trips students up:** Forgetting to switch calculator mode to radians, or confusing degree conversion formulas.
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - Arc length: $s = r\theta$ (with $\theta$ in radians).
  - Sector area: $A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$.
  - Triangle area: $A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\sin\theta$.
  - Segment area: $A = \frac{1}{2}r^2(\theta - \sin\theta)$.
  - Rapid conversion: $180^\circ = \pi\text{ rad}$, so $30^\circ = \pi/6$, $45^\circ = \pi/4$, $60^\circ = \pi/3$, $90^\circ = \pi/2$.
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `Arc length with r = 8 cm, θ = 1.5 rad` $\to$ `8 × 1.5 = 12 cm`
  - `Sector area with r = 6 cm, θ = 0.5 rad` $\to$ `0.5 × 36 × 0.5 = 9 cm²`
  - `Find θ (rad) if r = 5 cm and arc length is 15 cm` $\to$ `15 / 5 = 3 rad`
  - `Perimeter of sector with r = 10 cm, θ = 0.8 rad` $\to$ `2(10) + 10(0.8) = 28 cm`

---

### Topic 5: Trigonometric Exact Values & Identities (0606 Topic 10)
* **What trips students up:** Relying on a calculator for standard angles ($0, \pi/6, \pi/4, \pi/3, \pi/2$).
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - Hand trick / sequence $\sqrt{0}/2, \sqrt{1}/2, \sqrt{2}/2, \sqrt{3}/2, \sqrt{4}/2$ for $\sin(0, 30, 45, 60, 90^\circ)$.
  - Pythagorean identity: $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$, $1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta$, $1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta$.
  - ASTC quadrant sign rules for secondary solutions ($\pi - \theta, \pi + \theta, 2\pi - \theta$).
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `Exact value of 2 sin(π/6) cos(π/6) = sin(π/3)` $\to$ `√3/2 ≈ 0.87` or coefficient ratio
  - `If cos θ = 3/5 in quadrant 1, find tan θ` $\to$ `4/3`
  - `Number of solutions to sin(2x) = 0.5 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π` $\to$ `4`

---

### Topic 6: Arithmetic and Geometric Progressions (0606 Topic 12)
* **What trips students up:** Mixing up the $n$-th term formula with the sum formula, or finding the condition for convergence of infinite series.
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - AP $n$-th term: $u_n = a + (n-1)d$.
  - AP sum: $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(u_1 + u_n) = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$. Shortcut: Average of first and last $\times n$.
  - GP $n$-th term: $u_n = ar^{n-1}$.
  - GP sum to infinity (converges when $|r| < 1$): $S_\infty = \frac{a}{1 - r}$.
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `AP: a = 3, d = 4. Find 10th term` $\to$ `3 + 9 × 4 = 39`
  - `AP: Sum of first 20 integers from 1 to 20` $\to$ `20 × 21 / 2 = 210`
  - `GP: a = 18, r = 1/3. Find sum to infinity` $\to$ `18 / (2/3) = 27`
  - `GP: a = 24, S∞ = 32. Find common ratio r` $\to$ `1 - r = 24/32 = 3/4 ⟹ r = 0.25`

---

### Topic 7: Permutations and Combinations (0606 Topic 11)
* **What trips students up:** Choosing between ordering ($^n P_r$) and selecting ($^n C_r$), and handling grouped items.
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - Factorial shortcuts: $5! = 120$, $6! = 720$, $7! = 5040$.
  - Combinations: $^n C_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$. E.g., $^{10}C_2 = \frac{10 \times 9}{2} = 45$, $^{10}C_3 = \frac{10 \times 9 \times 8}{6} = 120$.
  - Grouping rule: treat $k$ items together as 1 item, multiply by $k!$ internal arrangements.
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `Evaluate 6P2` $\to$ `6 × 5 = 30`
  - `Evaluate 8C2` $\to$ `8 × 7 / 2 = 28`
  - `Evaluate 7C3` $\to$ `7 × 6 × 5 / 6 = 35`
  - `Number of ways to arrange letters in 'MATHS'` $\to$ `5! = 120`

---

### Topic 8: Basic Differentiation & Tangents (0606 Topic 14)
* **What trips students up:** Negative and fractional powers when differentiating $\frac{a}{x^n}$ or $\sqrt{x}$.
* **Speed shortcuts:**
  - Power rule: $\frac{d}{dx}[x^n] = n x^{n-1}$.
  - $\frac{d}{dx}[\frac{1}{x}] = -\frac{1}{x^2}$.
  - $\frac{d}{dx}[\sqrt{x}] = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$.
  - Chain rule mentally: $(ax + b)^n \implies a \cdot n(ax + b)^{n-1}$.
  - Gradient of normal: $m_{\text{normal}} = -\frac{1}{m_{\text{tangent}}}$.
* **Rapid Speed Drill Prompts:**
  - `Gradient of y = x² - 4x + 7 at x = 5` $\to$ `2(5) - 4 = 6`
  - `Gradient of y = 2x³ - 5x at x = 2` $\to$ `6(4) - 5 = 19`
  - `Stationary point x-coordinate for y = 2x² - 12x + 5` $\to$ `4x - 12 = 0 ⟹ x = 3`
  - `Gradient of normal to curve with tangent gradient m = 4` $\to$ `-0.25`

## 3. Recommended Phased Implementation Plan

| Phase | Topics | Key Speed Skills | Target Student Audience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** *(Implemented)* | Physics speed, Quadratic inequalities, Fast factorising, Decimals & large numbers | $v=d/t, F=ma, Ek, V=IR$; boundary roots; monic/non-monic quadratics; base 100 | Early Ad Maths & IGCSE Core |
| **Phase 2** *(Next)* | Indices & Surds, Remainder Theorem, AP & GP Series | Fractional power recall; $S_\infty = a/(1-r)$; synthetic evaluation | Mid Ad Maths (Term 1-2) |
| **Phase 3** *(Advanced)* | Radians & Circular measure, Logarithms, Combinations | $s=r\theta, A=0.5r^2\theta$; $\log_a b$; $^{n}C_2, ^{n}C_3$ | Final Ad Maths Preparation |
| **Phase 4** *(Calculus)* | Polynomial Differentiation & Stationary Points | Rapid power rules; finding turning points; tangent gradients | Exam Mock Drills (Paper 1 & 2) |
